COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help plan your visit to Brew & Bean.

Do you have high-speed Wi-Fi and power outlets for remote workers?

Yes! We provide complimentary 100 Mbps fiber Wi-Fi. Our mezzanine study corner and window bar tables feature universal charging ports under every seat.

Are pets allowed at Brew & Bean?

Absolutely. Our outdoor Garden Balcony is 100% pet-friendly. We even provide complimentary fresh water bowls and organic pet treats!

Do you offer vegan and non-dairy milk options?

Yes, we offer barista-formulated Oat Milk and Almond Milk for all our hot and iced espresso beverages. We also have gluten-free and eggless pastry selections.

Is car and two-wheeler parking available?

Yes, we have designated two-wheeler parking directly in front of the café, as well as valet assistance for four-wheelers during peak evening hours and weekends.
