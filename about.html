Our Story & Ethos

Crafting memorable coffee rituals and building a warm sanctuary for human connection since 2021.

HOW IT ALL BEGAN

Born From a Deep Love for the Humble Coffee Bean

Brew & Bean was founded with a singular conviction: that coffee is not just a morning caffeine fix, but a grounding ritual and a conduit for human connection. In a world moving at breakneck speed, we wanted to build a haven where people could slow down and savor the craftsmanship in every sip.

We began by partnering directly with smallholder coffee farmers in the misty hills of Chikmagalur and Coorg, ensuring ethical fair-trade practices and shade-grown cultivation that protects biodiversity.

“We don’t just pour espresso shots; we nurture conversations, provide quiet reading sanctuaries, and build lifelong friendships around freshly ground coffee.”

OUR JOURNEY

The Brew & Bean Timeline

Milestones that define our growth from a passion project to a bustling neighborhood haven.

2021

Brew & Bean Was Founded

Conceptualized during late-night cupping sessions by coffee lovers determined to bring specialty micro-lot beans directly from plantation estates.

2022

First Flagship Café Opened

Opened our doors at Coffee Lane in Bengaluru with 12 handcrafted wooden tables, a custom La Marzocco espresso machine, and an open baking counter.

2024

Expanded The Gourmet Menu

Introduced sourdough club sandwiches, artisanal Belgian chocolate desserts, artisanal tea infusions, and our signature cold brews.

2026

Growing Into a Community Hub

Celebrating over 10,000 satisfied patrons, weekly live acoustic events, weekend barista workshops, and sustainable compost initiatives.
